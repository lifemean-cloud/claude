# Impeccable Design Skill Configuration

## Core Design Principles
- **Anti-AI Slop**: Absolutely avoid generic dashboard layouts, over-styled colorful gradients, and cheap template shapes.
- **High-Fidelity Minimalism**: Emulate the premium, high-density digital craftsmanship of modern top-tier software tools like Notion, Linear, and Vercel. 
- **Typography & Space**: Use clean, refined typography (sans-serif, precise line-height) and generous, intentional white space (padding and margins).
- **Color Palette**: Restrict visually complex colors. Stick to a beautifully muted grayscale/monochrome foundation (slate, zinc, or neutral dark/light modes) with exactly ONE very subtle accent color for critical actions.
